Version 2.8
- Fixed the three cards on the Game Mode screen: the big numbers now shrink to fit and long titles wrap to two lines instead of being cut off.
- 6 new mods: Windows Game Mode, Mouse for games, Keyboard without popups, Shader cache, Monitor refresh rate and Windows startup. All but the shader cache have an Undo button.
- Fixed: the backup of "Windows for games" was lost when the program was closed, so Undo could not restore the old values.
