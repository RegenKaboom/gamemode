Version 2.6
- A notice after every update: a window with what is new, plus a line in the notices list.
- New game settings: keep the screen and PC awake while you play, and turn off fullscreen optimizations per game.
- Game Mode's own performance: choose Normal, Light or Minimal CPU use. Beep and update notice can be switched off.
