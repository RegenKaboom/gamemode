Version 2.5
- Languages: English, Portuguese and Spanish. Pick one in the installer or in Options > Accessibility.
- Light and dark themes, automatic (same as Windows) or your choice.
- Steam and Epic Games: Options > Import from Steam / Epic adds your installed games as profiles.
