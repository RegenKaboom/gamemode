Version 2.15
- New mod "System and privacy": turns off telemetry, suggested-app installs, Windows experimentation, Delivery Optimization P2P, voice activation and settings cloud sync, plus Win32 Priority and the service-split threshold. Needs administrator.
- New mod "Background tasks": turns off 4 Task Scheduler tasks (app-compat telemetry, SQM, diagnostics scanner, WinSAT). Needs administrator.
- New mod "Power and disk": turns off hibernation, USB selective suspend, and forces SSD TRIM on. Needs administrator.
- 4 new mods that don't need administrator: Windows nudges/suggestions off, Media Player usage tracking off, search without Bing suggestions, search without cloud.

- Mod novo "Tarefas em segundo plano": desliga 4 tarefas do Agendador de Tarefas (telemetria de compatibilidade, SQM, scanner de diagnóstico, WinSAT). Precisa de administrador.
- Mod novo "Energia e disco": desliga hibernação, suspensão seletiva de USB, e garante o TRIM do SSD ligado. Precisa de administrador.
- 4 mods novos que não precisam de administrador: avisos/sugestões do Windows, rastreamento do Media Player, busca sem sugestão do Bing e busca sem nuvem.

Version 2.13
- New mod: "Hardware-accelerated GPU scheduling" (HAGS) — lets the graphics card manage its own frame queue instead of Windows. Needs administrator and a restart to take effect.
- New mod: "Background services" — stops SysMain (Superfetch) and Windows Search while you play, and can turn both back on exactly as they were. Needs administrator.
- Both mods keep the same all-or-nothing safety as "Windows para jogos": if something fails partway, they undo themselves instead of leaving things half-changed.

Versão 2.13

- Mod novo: "Serviços em segundo plano" — para o SysMain (Superfetch) e a Pesquisa do Windows enquanto você joga, e religa os dois do jeito que estavam. Precisa de administrador.
- Os dois mods têm a mesma segurança "tudo ou nada" do "Windows para jogos": se algo falhar no meio do caminho, eles se desfazem sozinhos em vez de deixar a coisa pela metade.

Version 2.11
- Fixed: after opening the app from the tray icon, the window could reappear with no button on the Windows taskbar until you closed and reopened the whole program. It now forces the taskbar button back every time.
- Fixed: the "Windows para jogos" (win tweaks) mod could leave some of its 6 registry values changed and others not if one of them failed partway through Apply or Undo; it now rolls itself back automatically so it is always all-or-nothing. Undo is also more reliable if one of those settings already had an unusual value before you ever used the app.
